A research program on the learning dynamics of neural networks — mechanistic interpretability examined through a complex- and nonlinear-dynamical-systems lens. The question isn't did the model learn the task. It's how did learning happen.

Openone monorepo · MIScope

Most interpretability looks at a trained model and asks what it knows. Here we watch the model become — reading internal representation across a discrete series of checkpoints, and treating training itself as a trajectory with a shape.

The current focus is well-studied toy models — extended from isolated, final-state analysis to analysis over time. Toy models let us calibrate useful lenses while stress-testing the infrastructure that makes dynamics research on high-dimensional systems tractable. That infrastructure is MIScope.

The approach

Watch the path, not just the destination.

01 — The question

How did learning happen?

We hold a model still at many points in its training and compare. The interesting structure — phase transitions, grokking, representations forming — lives between checkpoints, in the movement.

02 — The method

A dynamical-systems lens.

Trajectories, dimensionality, eigenvalue migration, geometry over time. Tools from nonlinear dynamics, applied to model internals rather than to behavior alone.

03 — Why it matters

Diagnostics for model health.

A motivation was the Betley et al. (2025) result, where narrow fine-tuning produced broad misalignment. Understanding how representations are shaped over training could make model-health diagnostics — and more targeted steering — possible during production runs.

On safety and human–AI collaboration: these are real consequences of the work, not its front door. They're argued in prose, in the notes — never worn as a badge.

Downstream · the tooling

MIScope

The research infrastructure the program is built on: a Python library that frames analysis of model variants and their internals across checkpoints as an ETL-like pipeline, and standardizes its Plotly figures to reduce drift in interpretation. A Plotly Dash dashboard, run locally, sits on top.
